Project Overview

This project presents a professional E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard developed using Power BI to provide a comprehensive view of sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, regional contribution, payment preferences, discount patterns, and operational efficiency. The dashboard transforms 5,000+ transaction records into an interactive business intelligence solution that enables users to identify trends, compare performance, and derive actionable insights for business decision-making.

Business Objective

The primary objective of the project is to analyze e-commerce sales data and develop a centralized dashboard that enables management to monitor key performance indicators and understand the major drivers of revenue. The analysis focuses on identifying high-performing product categories and regions, evaluating customer purchasing patterns, understanding payment preferences, assessing discount effectiveness, monitoring customer satisfaction and delivery performance, and identifying changes in revenue over time.

Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard tracks critical business metrics including ₹5.11M in total revenue, 5,000 orders, 989 unique customers, ₹1,022 average order value, 20.2K total items sold, 2.97/5 average customer rating, 6.1 days average delivery time, and an average discount of 18%. These KPIs provide a consolidated snapshot of overall business performance and allow users to quickly assess sales, customer, and operational metrics.

Product Category Analysis

The Product Category analysis compares revenue generated across different product segments using a ranked horizontal bar chart. Electronics is the leading category with approximately ₹1.83M in revenue, followed by Clothing at approximately ₹1.53M, while Home and Beauty contribute comparatively lower revenue. This analysis helps identify the categories that have the strongest impact on overall sales and can support inventory planning, marketing allocation, and category-level growth strategies.

Regional Sales Analysis

The Regional Sales analysis evaluates revenue performance across different geographical markets. The West region is the highest-performing market with approximately ₹1.35M in revenue, followed by North, South, and East. The analysis enables management to identify high-value markets, compare regional performance, and develop targeted strategies for market expansion and customer acquisition.

Payment Method Analysis

The Payment Method analysis presents the contribution of different payment channels to total revenue. Card payments contribute approximately 46% of revenue, followed by COD at 35% and Wallet payments at 19%. This analysis provides insight into customer payment preferences and can support decisions related to digital payment adoption, payment experience optimization, and transaction strategy.

Discount Analysis

The Discount analysis categorizes transactions based on discount levels to understand the distribution of promotional activity across the business. The dashboard evaluates low, medium, high, and very high discount bands, enabling management to assess how heavily discounting is being used and identify opportunities to optimize promotional strategies without unnecessarily reducing revenue margins.

Regional Category Heatmap

The Regional Category Heatmap provides a detailed Region × Product Category analysis to identify the areas where specific product categories perform strongly. Electronics demonstrates strong revenue contribution across multiple regions, while Beauty records comparatively lower sales. This visualization enables more granular decision-making by highlighting category-level opportunities within individual geographic markets.

Sales Trend Analysis

The Sales Trend analysis tracks annual revenue performance across the available period from 2022 to 2035. The dashboard identifies 2033 as the peak revenue year, followed by a significant decline toward the end of the dataset period. This visualization helps management understand long-term revenue movement and identify periods requiring further investigation into demand, pricing, product mix, customer behavior, or market conditions.

Customer Experience Analysis

Customer experience is evaluated using customer ratings, with the dataset reporting an average rating of 2.97 out of 5. The relatively low rating indicates an opportunity to investigate factors affecting customer satisfaction, including product quality, delivery experience, service responsiveness, pricing, and post-purchase support.

Delivery Performance Analysis

The dashboard monitors average delivery performance, with an overall average delivery time of approximately 6.1 days. Delivery performance is also compared across regions, allowing the business to identify markets with relatively slower fulfilment and investigate potential logistics, inventory, or supply-chain inefficiencies.

Discount and Customer Rating Relationship

A correlation analysis between discount levels and customer ratings produces a correlation coefficient of approximately 0.01, indicating an extremely weak linear relationship between the two variables. This suggests that increasing discounts alone is unlikely to substantially improve customer satisfaction and that businesses should focus on broader factors such as product quality, service, delivery reliability, and customer support.

Business Insights

The analysis indicates that Electronics and the West region are major contributors to overall revenue, making them important areas for continued investment and growth. At the same time, the relatively low customer rating and 6.1-day average delivery time highlight opportunities to improve the overall customer experience. The payment analysis indicates a strong preference for card-based transactions, while the weak relationship between discounts and customer ratings suggests that promotional strategies should be designed around profitability and customer value rather than relying solely on higher discounts.

Business Recommendations

The business should prioritize high-performing categories such as Electronics through targeted marketing, optimized inventory allocation, and cross-selling opportunities. Regional strategies should focus on strengthening the West market while identifying growth opportunities in other regions. Customer experience initiatives should address the factors contributing to the 2.97/5 rating, while fulfilment processes should be reviewed to reduce delivery times. Discount strategies should also be optimized based on profitability and customer behavior rather than assuming that higher discounts automatically improve satisfaction.

Tools and Technologies

The project was developed using Microsoft Power BI, Power Query, DAX, and CSV-based data. Power Query was used for data preparation and transformation, while DAX was used to develop calculated measures and KPIs. Power BI was used to create interactive visualizations, analytical dashboards, and business intelligence reports, with emphasis on data modeling, KPI development, exploratory analysis, and professional data storytelling.

Project Workflow

The project followed an end-to-end business intelligence workflow beginning with raw e-commerce transaction data, followed by data cleaning and transformation, data modeling, creation of DAX measures, exploratory data analysis, dashboard development, and interpretation of business insights. This approach ensured that the final dashboard was not limited to visualization but also provided meaningful analytical conclusions and actionable recommendations.

Key Learning Outcomes

This project provided practical experience in data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, dashboard design, data visualization, and business analysis. It also strengthened the ability to translate raw transactional data into management-level insights and present complex information through a structured and professional Power BI dashboard.

Project Outcome

The final dashboard provides a centralized analytical view of e-commerce performance and enables users to move from high-level KPIs to detailed category, regional, payment, discount, customer, and time-based analysis. The project demonstrates the application of Power BI as a business intelligence tool for converting transactional data into actionable insights that can support sales optimization, customer experience improvement, operational efficiency, and strategic decision-making.
